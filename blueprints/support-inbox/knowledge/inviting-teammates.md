# Inviting teammates

Admins invite people from Settings → Members → Invite. Each invite is a link that works for 7 days. If an invite has expired, click **Resend** next to the person's name.

Invited people who don't see the email should check their spam folder, and ask their IT team to allow mail from our domain.

Each plan includes a number of seats (Starter: 3, Team: 20, Business: unlimited). Inviting someone past the limit asks the admin to add a seat.
