# Plans, invoices and billing

- **Changing plans:** Settings → Billing → Change plan. Upgrades apply at once and are prorated. Downgrades apply at the end of the current billing period.
- **Invoices:** every invoice is under Settings → Billing → Invoices, as a PDF. To add a VAT number or a billing address, edit **Billing details** on the same page; future invoices use it.
- **Failed payments:** we retry a failed card three times over seven days and email the account owner each time. Update the card under Settings → Billing → Payment method.
- **Refunds:** refunds are handled by the billing team, not by support. Tell the customer that a person from billing will reply within two business days.
