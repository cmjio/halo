# Resetting your password

1. Go to the sign-in page and click **Forgot password?**
2. Enter the email address you signed up with.
3. Open the email we send you (check spam if it doesn't arrive within five minutes) and click the link. It works for one hour.
4. Choose a new password of at least 12 characters.

If your company signs you in with SSO (Google, Microsoft or Okta), you don't have a password with us: reset it with your company's IT team instead.

Support staff can never see or set your password, and will never ask for it.
