# Shipping and refunds policy

These are the rules. Replies must not promise anything they don't allow.

## Delivery times
- Standard shipping: 3–5 business days after the order ships.
- Express shipping: 1–2 business days after the order ships.
- Orders ship within 2 business days of being placed.

## Late orders
- An order is **late** when it has been in transit longer than its delivery time above.
- Up to 7 business days in transit: apologise, share the tracking link, and ask the customer to wait 2 more business days.
- More than 7 business days in transit: we open a trace with the carrier. Say we have opened one and that we will reply within 2 business days.
- More than 15 business days in transit: the parcel counts as lost. Offer a free replacement or a full refund, the customer's choice.

## Refunds
- Unopened items can be returned within 30 days for a full refund.
- Never promise a refund for a late order unless it counts as lost (above).
- Never offer discount codes. Only the support lead can.
